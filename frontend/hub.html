How it works

Three steps, about a minute.

You stay on the job board the whole time. Nothing connects to your account and nothing runs in the background.

1

Describe yourself once

Skills, minimum rate, keywords you want and keywords you don't. Saved in your browser, reused on every board.

2

Paste what you're looking at

Select the job list, copy, paste. Menus, footers and banners are stripped out automatically.

3

Work the top of the list

Scores, verdicts and reasons, sorted highest first. Export to CSV when you want a record.

🔑

One licence, every board

Your monthly evaluations are shared across all supported boards — spend them wherever you're searching that week. No separate purchase per board, and your profile carries over automatically.

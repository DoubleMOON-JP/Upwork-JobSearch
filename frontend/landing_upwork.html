How it works

Three steps, about a minute.

Copy from Upwork and paste into MOONpicker. Nothing connects to your account and nothing runs in the background.

1

Set your preferences

Skills, minimum rate, keywords you want, keywords you don't. It's saved in your browser, so you fill it in once and forget it.

2

Paste your search results

Copy the whole Upwork search results page using Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C (⌘+A then ⌘+C on Mac), and paste it into MOONpicker. The menus, footers and banners get stripped out automatically.

3

Review top scoring jobs

Every job comes back with a score, a verdict, and the reason behind it — sorted highest first. Export to CSV when you want to keep a record.

Why it helps

Connects are finite. Attention is worse.

The cost of a bad application isn't the Connects — it's the twenty minutes you spent writing the proposal.

Scored on your terms, not a generic fit

Your minimum rate, your skills, your dealbreakers. A $20/hr job scores badly for you and well for someone else — that's the point.

Reads the client, not just the job

Payment verified, past spend, proposal count, how long it's been up. The signals that predict whether a post is worth answering.

Tells you why, every time

Each score comes with the specific factor that drove it. Disagree with one and you'll know immediately which input to adjust.

Ask for what you want

A free-text instruction goes straight to the AI. Weight long-term contracts higher, ignore fixed-price work, get reasons in your own language.

Questions

Before you sign up.

Which platforms are supported?

Upwork and Freelancer.com.

Is MOONpicker free?

Free through December 31, 2026. The free period may be extended. Register with your email to receive a licence key.

Does MOONpicker apply for me?

No. MOONpicker never submits proposals and never signs in to your job board account. You decide and apply yourself.

Does this break Upwork's terms of service?

No. There is no extension, no scraping and no automated access to Upwork. You copy text yourself, the same way you would copy it into a notes app, and paste it here. Your Upwork account is never touched.

What counts as one evaluation?

Each press of Evaluate Search Results counts as one evaluation, however many jobs you paste. You can evaluate once every 2 minutes.

Can I get the reasons in a language other than English?

Yes. Write the request in the instruction box — for example, asking for reasons in Japanese — and the scoring follows it.

What happens when I run out of evaluations?

Scoring pauses until your next monthly cycle begins. Your profile and settings stay exactly as they were.

How do I get my license key?

Your licence key is sent by email after registration. Enter it on the app’s licence-key screen to begin.
